About Us

Haki Restaurant opened in 2015 with one wood-fired grill and a simple idea: good food takes time, and it tastes better when it is shared.

We cook with seasonal ingredients from local farms and bake our bread fresh every morning. Whether you are here for a family lunch or a late dinner with friends, we will save you a seat.
